My Three Traits

Leadership

Leadership is one of my strongest personality traits because I am comfortable taking initiative, guiding others, and working toward a common goal. I believe that leadership is important in both school and the workplace because it encourages teamwork, cooperation, and responsibility. For example, during group assignments, I often help to organize tasks, encourage participation, and ensure that everyone contributes. As a result, leadership has helped me to develop stronger communication skills, improve my ability to work with others, and become more confident in expressing my ideas.

Determination

Determination is an important part of my personality because it motivates me to set goals and work hard to achieve them. In my academic life, I understand that success requires dedication, discipline, and consistent effort. Therefore, I try to stay focused on my studies, complete assignments on time, and prepare adequately for examinations. Even when schoolwork becomes stressful, I remind myself of the goals that I want to achieve. Consequently, determination helps me to remain motivated, manage my responsibilities, and strive for academic success.

Persistence

Persistence is another strong personality trait that helps me to continue working toward my goals, even when I face challenges. Sometimes, I may find certain topics difficult to understand or may not achieve the results I expected. However, instead of giving up, I try to learn from my mistakes, seek help when necessary, and find better ways to improve. Furthermore, persistence has taught me the importance of patience and resilience in my academic journey. As a result, I have become more willing to overcome obstacles, learn from setbacks, and continue working toward improvement.

My Hero

My Favorite Quote

"There is no expiration date on personal growth, education, or reinvention."

Background

My hero is Khadine Wilkinson, popularly known as Miss Kitty. She is a Jamaican media personality, attorney-at-law, motivational speaker, and entrepreneur. She became well known through radio and television because of her energetic, outgoing personality and distinctive presence. Many Jamaicans came to know her as the “Fluffy Diva” when she hosted and served as a judge on Magnum Kings and Queens of Dancehall. Beyond her successful career in entertainment, Miss Kitty has also demonstrated a commitment to education and personal development by pursuing further academic qualifications. Her journey reflects the importance of continuous learning and the willingness to grow beyond one's existing achievements.

Why Is She My Hero

Khadine Wilkinson is my hero because she represents ambition, independence, confidence, and women's empowerment. She encourages women to become the best versions of themselves by investing in their personal growth, pursuing their goals, and recognizing their worth. Beyond her professional achievements, I admire how she promotes femininity, grace, and poise while encouraging women to carry themselves with confidence and dignity without compromising their ambitions.

One message of hers that particularly resonates with me is the idea that she does not simply want to marry a wealthy man and depend on him; she also wants to become a successful, wealthy woman herself. To me, this represents the importance of financial independence and building a life that allows you to stand on your own. I admire the idea that a woman can desire a successful partner while also working toward her own success, rather than relying entirely on someone else to provide the life that she wants.

Additionally, I admire Miss Kitty's determination to start her Bachelor of Laws (LLB) at around age 40 and complete her legal education by 45, despite already having an established career in entertainment. Her journey challenged my perception of success and the belief that certain achievements must happen by a particular age. She has taught me that everyone's timeline is different and that we should not limit ourselves because of age or fear of starting over. The years will pass regardless of what we choose to do, so why not spend them pursuing something meaningful? This perspective has encouraged me to embrace new possibilities, challenge expectations, and understand that my current choices do not have to define my entire future.

Ultimately, Miss Kitty is my hero because she embodies the kind of woman I aspire to become: educated, independent, poised, confident, and continually evolving. She inspires me to embrace my femininity without compromising my ambition, invest in myself, and remain open to new opportunities. Most importantly, she has taught me not to fear starting over because of my age or remain on a path that I have no passion for simply because I have already invested time and effort into it. Her example has shown me that I am not obligated to continue pursuing something that no longer aligns with my interests simply because it is what I have always known. It is never too late to explore new possibilities, change direction, or pursue a different dream. Through her journey, I have learned that resilience means having the courage to grow beyond my comfort zone and allowing myself to evolve, even when life does not unfold according to the timeline I once imagined.

LLMs: A Tool, Not a Crutch

To me, the statement “LLMs must be used as a tool, not a crutch” means that AI should support my learning rather than replace my thinking. As a Cybersecurity student, relying on AI to complete assignments without understanding the answers may help me earn a grade, but it will not develop the knowledge and skills that I need for my future career.

Although LLMs offer many benefits, overreliance on them can negatively affect learning. Bastani et al. (2025) found that students using an unrestricted AI assistant performed worse on subsequent tests without AI assistance. Similarly, Kosmyna et al. (2025) demonstrated that relying heavily on AI during writing tasks can reduce cognitive engagement and active memory retrieval. Furthermore, LLMs can generate convincing but inaccurate information, making verification essential (Ji et al., 2022). These findings highlight the importance of thinking critically rather than depending entirely on AI-generated answers.

However, LLMs can improve my learning when used responsibly. At home, in the library, or during independent study sessions, I can use them to simplify difficult concepts, generate practice questions, and provide hints when solving problems. When preparing for examinations, I can use them to test my understanding and identify weaknesses. During my practical sessions, I can use them to explain security concepts, review code, and identify potential errors. Additionally, when completing assignments, I can use them to improve grammar and clarify misunderstandings, provided that I verify the information and follow UTech's academic integrity guidelines.

Ultimately, I believe that AI is beneficial when it helps me become an independent learner. I ask myself, “Can I explain this concept or solve this problem without AI afterward?” If not, I need more practice. LLMs should strengthen my abilities rather than replace them because education should develop independent thinking, problem-solving skills, and genuine understanding.
